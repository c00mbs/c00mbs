## What I'm building

**CareerOS.ai** (part of the Become ecosystem) turns your work history into one trustworthy career record — then matches you to verified-real jobs and proves where every application stands.

- **TRACK** — know what is happening in your career
- **TRACE** — know what happened, and prove it
- **TRUST** — know what is credible
